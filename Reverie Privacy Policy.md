# Reverie Privacy Policy

Nov 16, 2026 · @Simon

Reverie is a private dream journal. Your dreams stay on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account. The developer never receives them.

This policy explains what Reverie stores, where it goes, and the choices you have. It applies to the Reverie app for iPhone (listed on the App Store as "Reverie: Dream Recorder"), made by Simon Foster.

In short:

- There is no Reverie account, sign-in or server.
- Reverie has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and never sells data.
- Dreams are only sent to an outside AI service if you choose an external artwork engine and enter your own API key.
- Apple Health data is read only on your device. It is never written, shared or stored in iCloud.

## What Reverie stores

Everything Reverie keeps is stored on your device. Items marked "iCloud" are also copied to your private iCloud account when iCloud sync is on.

| Information | Why | Where it's kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice recordings of your dreams | So you can replay them | Device; iCloud if "Include Recordings" is on |
| Transcripts, titles, moods and themes | The core of your journal | Device; iCloud |
| Interpretations and follow-up conversations | Reading your dreams | Device; iCloud |
| Dream artwork | Illustrating each dream | Device; iCloud |
| Tags you add, such as lucid or nightmare | Filtering and patterns | Device; iCloud |
| Sleep from the night before a dream | Showing the night behind each dream | Device only |
| Your first name, if you enter it | Greeting you | Device only |
| Archetype test results | Context for interpretations | Device only |
| Your hemisphere (north or south) | Showing the moon correctly | Device only |
| API keys for external artwork engines | Painting with your account | Device Keychain only, never synced |
| A summary of your latest dream | The Home Screen widget | Device only; hidden while Face ID lock is on |

## How AI features handle your dreams

By default, every AI feature in Reverie runs on your device with Apple's built-in technology. Your recording and transcript are never sent to an outside AI service.

- **Transcription.** Apple's on-device speech recognition turns your recording into text. If on-device recognition isn't available for your language, your device may use Apple's speech recognition service, under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Interpretations and conversations.** Apple Intelligence reads your dream and answers follow-up questions on your device.
- **Artwork.** Image Playground paints your dream on your device. If it isn't available, Reverie draws a composition from the dream's mood instead.

### Optional external artwork engines

In Settings you can choose to paint artwork with OpenAI's GPT Image or Google's Nano Banana (Gemini), using an API key from your own account. Reverie asks for your consent before the first use.

When you use one of these engines, Reverie sends that provider a short painting brief for each dream: the dream's emotional theme, its mood, and a description of the scene to paint. Reverie never sends your recording, transcript, sleep data, name or other dreams.

The request goes directly from your device to the provider, under your account and that provider's terms and privacy policy:

- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Gemini API Additional Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)

To stop, switch back to Image Playground in Settings. Your API key is stored in your device's Keychain and is removed if you clear it.

## Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Reverie, the app reads only your sleep analysis: time in bed, time asleep and, with Apple Watch, sleep stages. Reverie never writes anything to Health.

- Sleep data is used only to show the night behind each dream and to find links between your sleep and your dreams.
- A summary of the night before a dream is saved with that dream on your device.
- Health data is never stored in iCloud, never sent to an external artwork engine, and never shared with anyone, including the developer.
- Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

To stop, turn off Apple Health in Reverie's Settings, or remove Reverie's access in the Health app under Sharing → Apps. Dreams keep the sleep summary they were saved with until you delete them.

## iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is on, which it is by default, Reverie keeps your journal in step across your devices using your private iCloud database. Only you can access it; the developer cannot.

- Each dream's text, interpretation, conversation and tags are stored as encrypted fields. Artwork and recordings are stored as encrypted iCloud files.
- With Advanced Data Protection turned on for your Apple Account, this data is end-to-end encrypted.
- Recordings use the most space. You can stop uploading them with "Include Recordings" in Settings → iCloud.
- Sleep data, your name, archetype results and API keys never sync.

iCloud is provided by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [iCloud Terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/).

To stop syncing, turn off "Sync with iCloud" in Settings. Your journal stays on your device. To remove Reverie's data from iCloud, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud → Manage Storage (or Storage) → Reverie, and delete its data.

## Other permissions

Reverie asks for each permission only when a feature needs it. You can change any of them in the Settings app under Apps → Reverie.

| Permission | What it's used for |
| --- | --- |
| Microphone | Recording your dream while you speak |
| Speech Recognition | Turning your recording into text |
| Location (approximate, once) | Working out whether you're in the northern or southern hemisphere for the moon calendar. Only the hemisphere is saved; your location is not. |
| Photos (add only) | Saving dream cards you choose to share. Reverie can't see your photo library. |
| Face ID or passcode | The optional app lock. Apple handles authentication; Reverie never sees your face data or passcode. |
| Notifications | The optional morning reminder, scheduled on your device |

## Sharing and third parties

Reverie does not sell, rent or share your information. The developer does not collect it.

- Reverie contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
- The only outside services Reverie contacts are Apple's (iCloud, and speech recognition when on-device recognition isn't available) and, only if you choose one, OpenAI or Google for artwork.
- Dream cards, exported journals and anything else you share leave Reverie only when you share them yourself.
- If you join a TestFlight beta, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

## Your choices and deleting your data

You control everything Reverie keeps, because it lives on your device and in your own iCloud.

- **Delete a dream** from the journal. This removes its recording and artwork and, with sync on, removes it from iCloud and your other devices.
- **Export your journal** as a text file from Settings → Your journal.
- **Delete everything** by deleting the app, and removing its iCloud data as described under iCloud sync.
- Reverie keeps your data until you delete it. Nothing is kept anywhere the developer can reach.

## Children

Reverie is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
